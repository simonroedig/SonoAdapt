Our user study is a three-factor within-subjects design with three systems (Baseline-Earcon, Baseline-Speech, AudioAdapt), two notification urgency levels (Low, High), and two scenarios (mental arithmetic in quiet, Interview with speech), for 12 conditions per participant. Each condition is realized through two notification instances, yielding six notification runs per scenario. The two scenarios were selected to span variations in soundscape, task load, and social setting to demonstrate the adaptive system across everyday contexts. We conducted an a priori power analysis using G*Power~\cite{faul2007g} (version 3.1.9.6) to determine the required sample size. Following effect-size conventions\citet{yatani2016effect}, we set the anticipated effect size to Cohen’s $f = 0.25$. To account for the two scenario analyses, we applied a Bonferroni correction, yielding a per-test significance level of $\alpha = 0.05/2 = 0.025$, with a desired statistical power of $1 - \beta = 0.80$. We assumed a correlation among repeated measures of $\rho = 0.7$, on the basis that within-subject Likert ratings on the same construct across conditions typically show substantial rater consistency, and a nonsphericity correction of $\epsilon = 1$. The power analysis indicated a minimum total sample size of 21 participants (achieved power $1 - \beta = 0.818$, noncentrality parameter $\lambda = 13.13$, critical $F = 4.05$ with $(2, 40)$ degrees of freedom). The study was approved by the local ethics review board.

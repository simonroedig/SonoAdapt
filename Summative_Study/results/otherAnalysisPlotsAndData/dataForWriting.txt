\subsection{Manipulation Check: Perceived Urgency and Importance}
\label{sec:manipulation_check}

To verify that the notification messages were perceived as intended, participants rated the urgency and importance of each message on a 0--100 slider scale after each trial. Table~\ref{tab:perceived_urgency} summarises these ratings per message.

Low-urgency/low-importance messages, which described mundane daily activities, were perceived accordingly, with mean urgency and importance ratings of $M = 3.0$ and $M = 4.6$, respectively. High-urgency/high-importance messages, which described situations requiring immediate action (e.g., emergencies, time-sensitive requests), were rated substantially higher ($M = 81.8$ for urgency, $M = 80.4$ for importance). This confirms that the notification content successfully conveyed the intended urgency/importance levels.
